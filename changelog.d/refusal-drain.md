kind: A refusal now reaches the client (unread request bodies are drained)
---
- **Fix, every adapter (httpant-server; PM TASK from configurant's probe):** a handler (or a limit) that answered HTTP/1.1 without reading the request body, then had the connection closed with that body unread, lost its answer to a TCP reset on Windows: a refused `GET` with a 1 MB or 5 MB body showed the client `ConnectionAborted` and no status, 30 of 30 times (50 B and 70 KB bodies were answered). The server now reads and discards the unread body before it sends the answer: up to `Limits::max_drain_bytes` (new, default 8 MiB, `0` turns it off) within `Limits::drain_timeout` (new, default 5 s); a body not finished by then makes the answer say `Connection: close`. One wrapper on the service every adapter runs (`ServeOptions::wrap`, new module `drain`), so all six adapters behave alike. Not drained: HTTP/2, a request with `Expect`, a declared length over the cap, a body something still holds (a response that streams it back), no body at all. **Not marked Breaking, PM to rule:** `Limits` gains two public fields, but it is `non_exhaustive`, so nobody outside the crate could build it by literal; the visible change is that a refusal now takes as long as its unread body takes to arrive, up to the 5 s timeout, where it used to be answered at once.
- Conformance case 20 `refusal-delivered` (a handler's `403` for a GET with a 1.5 MiB body, and the body limit's `413` for a declared 5 MiB, to a client that writes the whole body before it reads) with the mutant `no-drain`, which fails it with "no answer reached the client" on Windows; unit tests in `drain.rs` for the cap, the timeout path, Expect, HTTP/2, declared length, no body and a handler that reads. Limit, stated: a body larger than the cap is still cut off with `Connection: close`, and a client still sending may not see the answer; refusals the web library makes itself before the service runs (a malformed head) are not covered.
