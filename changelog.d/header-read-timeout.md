kind: Limits::header_read_timeout (30 s) on hyper, actix-web and salvo
---
- **New limit (ROADMAP row 17, third slice; httpant#73):** `Limits::header_read_timeout` (default **30 s**, `Duration::ZERO` = off, off in `Limits::unlimited()`): a client that starts a request head and does not finish it in that time is cut off. Set on each library's own head timer, because HTTPAnt cannot see a head before the library has parsed it: hyper's `header_read_timeout` (with a tokio timer, which the adapter did not have), actix-web's `client_request_timeout` (plain and TLS listeners), salvo's `FuseConfig::http1_header_timeout`. **Not enforced on axum, poem and warp:** they give HTTPAnt no way to set one (axum and warp run hyper-util's builder with no hook; poem has only an activity-based `idle_timeout`, which would also end a quiet long-poll), so they keep their own default, which is none (measured); declared `header_read_timeout: No` (new `Capabilities::header_read_timeout`) and held by the case.
- **Changes defaults:** hyper had no head timeout and now has 30 s; **actix-web had 5 s and now has 30 s** (one setting everywhere it is enforced; lower `Limits::header_read_timeout` to keep the old figure); salvo was already 30 s. hyper and salvo also close an idle keep-alive connection at the same time (measured: 3.0 s at a 3 s setting); actix-web's idle keep-alive stays its own 5 s.
- Conformance case 23 `header-read-timeout` with the mutant `no-header-read-timeout`: a head that stops half way is cut off (closed or `408`) within the timeout on the three adapters that enforce it, a head finished in two pieces inside the timeout is served, and an adapter that declares `No` must still be waiting 2.5 s later. Passes on all six adapters on Windows, and on Ubuntu (WSL) for the reference. `docs/LIMITS-BY-ADAPTER.md` now has all three limits and both probes (40 s with no limit set; 12 s with a 3 s setting). Not tested: the head timeout on the TLS listeners (case is cleartext). Not published.
